# YourMove strategic roadmap through v1.0.0

**Status:** Adopted November 20, 2026 as the strategic product direction for an Apple Silicon Mac product. This document is the canonical roadmap. It establishes product direction and release sequence only: individual release scope, implementation and publication each require separate owner authorization, and a roadmap milestone is not a task assignment. Execution work is tracked in Linear (see [`AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md) and [`docs/BACKLOG.md`](BACKLOG.md)). The October 23, 2026 initial release roadmap is preserved as a historical document at [`docs/roadmaps/archive/2026-10-23-initial-release-roadmap.md`](roadmaps/archive/2026-10-23-initial-release-roadmap.md).

## 1. Product thesis

Keep the signature week. An applicant prepares two related applications, submits personally, hears back about the first, and returns to the exact material and commitment that matter today. This gives every surface a reason to exist. The strongest product hypothesis is that an applicant tailoring several applications will return for the connection between preparation and hiring progress. It still needs observation with intended users.

**Do not rebuild what already exists.** Immutable résumé revisions, role-family routing, application-only writing, Previous Writing and a Go-owned next-step suggestion already exist. Spending releases introducing them again would leave the hardest user question unanswered. After a week away, can I tell which file belongs to which application and what still needs my judgment? Only historical résumé retrieval is committed in advance in v0.5.0, and any additional preparation clarity must first be observed failing in a real two-application workflow (see §5, v0.5.0). Give the freed capacity to grounded conversational retrieval across those records. [Résumé operations][resumes] · [Writing reuse][equiv] · [Workspace decisions][workspace]

**Lead with one new reason to come back.** Ship a visual interview and next action in v0.4. Make the first typed conversation useful across applications in v0.6, add a confirmed local write in v0.7, then qualify optional speech on those same reads and that same write in v0.8. Conversation here means the applicant can phrase a supported intention in their own words, answer a clarification, refer back to “that role” or “Thursday's screen,” and follow up — while every answer stays grounded in local records with a route to the evidence and every change stays a bounded, confirmed Go operation. Interface polish and installed upgrade continuity start with the first changed workflow. This sequence gives an independent developer one new high-uncertainty problem at a time and preserves the visual, conversational and voice destination.

## 4. Strategic decisions and rationale

1. **Choose the interview plus one next action.** Record the scheduled screen and an optional applicant-set action with a due date. Allow correction, completion and a short outcome. Waiting for an employer has no deadline until the applicant chooses one. Surface due and overdue work from Opportunities, with a link to History. Keep undated actions available on the application. Defer a separate Home screen, general tasks and a hiring pipeline. This selects concept B from the November 17 planning record. [v0.4 alternatives][v4plan]
2. **Make v0.5 prove its value before it builds.** Start with two similarly named files and two related jobs on the current build and watch where the applicant actually hesitates, misidentifies a file, or cannot find outstanding work. Commit in advance only to the gap already visible in the code: a submission record names the résumé that was used but offers no direct route from that record to its verified bytes, especially once the version is archived or another file shares its name. Any further preparation-clarity work enters v0.5 only for friction that observation reproduces. Defer file comparison, résumé parsing and a global readiness score. The existing profile, Answer Library and Career Evidence remain available throughout. [Revisions][resumes] · [Go preparation summary][workspace] · [Application writing][writing]
3. **Spend the recovered capacity on everyday questions asked naturally.** Ground “What should I follow up on?” in unresolved actions. Ground “Help me prepare for Thursday” in the scheduled event, job description, recorded résumé and retained writing. For “last week,” distinguish submission, answer save and hiring activity. A passive visit may have no record. Ask which meaning the person intends or show dated matches with their basis. The applicant should not need to learn the app's phrasing: varied wording for the same supported intention resolves to the same grounded answer, a follow-up such as “What did I send them?” continues from the role just discussed, and an ambiguous request earns a short clarification rather than a guess. This supports ordinary language without an activity logger, transcript archive or speculative ranking of employer interest.
4. **Keep one path to a consequence.** A direct form, a confirmed language proposal and a corrected voice transcript call the same Go operation on the same local records. Speech first yields editable text. A useful response shows the target, the actual saved result and a way to correct it. Keep Wails 3, the existing protected transport, SQLite writer and managed document store. A new package, model provider or microphone service needs a demonstrated job within one vertical slice. [Feature ownership][ownership] · [Existing protected routes][routes]
5. **Define conversation narrowly enough to build, broadly enough to feel natural.** A supported intention is a bounded product operation — retrieve due work, retrieve one application's material, record one interview and linked action — not an open-ended agent task. Naturalness lives in how that intention is expressed and refined: paraphrase, relative dates, references to the current role or event, clarification when a detail is missing or two records match, and follow-up questions within the current interaction. Flexibility lives in interpretation; authority stays in Go validation, confirmation and the saved record. No unrestricted tool use, long-lived chat memory, general MCP infrastructure or parallel state model is required, and none is introduced. Short-lived context for the current conversation — the selected role, event and last result — is disposable UI state, discarded without loss because the durable facts already live in the workspace.
6. **Let voice prove retrieval as well as capture.** The smallest voice experience that demonstrates the multimodal direction is one opt-in push-to-talk entry over two already-proven operations: ask aloud what needs attention and hear or read the same grounded answer typing would give, and dictate a short invitation that becomes the same editable proposal typing would produce. Retrieval is the lower-risk half — it changes nothing — and it is the half an applicant returning after days away is most likely to use hands-busy. If installed evidence shows spoken retrieval works but spoken capture does not yet correct reliably, ship retrieval alone and hold the capture claim rather than promising comprehensive voice control. Always-on listening, spoken navigation, spoken Fill and spoken submission declarations stay excluded in every case.

## 5. Release roadmap v0.4.0–v1.0.0

The acceptance journeys below are proposed release evidence. Use isolated synthetic workspaces for engineering qualification. At each restart, fully quit and reopen the same executable on the same workspace without reseeding. Applicant research can use realistic fictional roles and materials. No employer submission is required to qualify the desktop product.

### v0.4.0 · Return for the next hiring commitment

**Promise and experience.** From a specific locally recorded application in History, schedule a screen, set a dated preparation action, and later record what happened. On returning to Opportunities, open the due item and see who set it, which role it belongs to and why it appears today.

**Reuse and build.** Use the job and submission IDs, existing History and Opportunities, and the protected Go read/write path. Add the smallest persistent hiring record needed for the event, editable date/time and zone, short outcome, and optional next action with its own due/completed state. A date-only due day should remain that local day. Save linked edits together. Derive due work from the saved facts. Keep the existing Focus/Saved/History membership and the employer-transfer guard on terminal jobs. [Job placement][jobs] · [Workspace snapshot][workspace] · [Submission correction][sub]

**Dependency, limits and effort.** Medium complexity concentrated in record identity and correction. Select the originating submission explicitly if there is more than one. A stage-only Applied job can open its status and use the existing manual recording workflow if the applicant chooses. When someone corrects a mistaken declaration, show the affected hiring work before confirmation. Retain it on the same opportunity, mark the former link as corrected and preserve the due action. Never silently attach it to another submission. Include hiring work in the existing explicit whole-job deletion disclosure and transaction. Exclude chat, speech, external documents, notifications and calendar sync. [Correction UI][subui] · [Deletion owner][delete]

**Acceptance.** On an upgraded synthetic v0.3 workspace, use a recorded role whose posting has closed. Save Thursday’s screen and Wednesday’s preparation action. Quit, reopen, find the due item outside Focus, reschedule the interview, finish the action and enter a short outcome. History still shows Applied and the source still shows closed. On another test record correct a mistaken declaration with a linked event. The person sees what was corrected, can still find their note, and sees no invented submission or duplicate action after retry.

### v0.5.0 · Recover the first application's résumé — and fix only what a second application proves is unclear

**Promise and experience.** From A's submission history, open the exact résumé that application used, even after newer revisions exist. Everything else in this release is earned by observation: if preparing a second, related application on the current build already works clearly, v0.5 ships as this retrieval fix alone and the roadmap moves on sooner.

**What already exists — do not rebuild.** Native revision import already creates an immutable new version, archives its predecessor, carries routing forward and leaves job assignments intact. Role-family routing and selectors, the candidate profile, reviewed Career Evidence, Previous Writing with application-only edits, per-job drafts, the Go preparation/readiness summary and continuation decisions are all shipped. v0.5 claims none of them as new work. [Revisions][resumes] · [Go preparation summary][workspace] · [Application writing][writing]

**Committed build — the one demonstrated gap.** The submission row already stores the résumé ID, name and hash, and a verified download route already exists in the Documents library — but the submission history itself only prints “date · Résumé: name” as text, with no route from that record to its file. Connect the submission row to a Go-verified historical file action that works when the version is archived, and present version, role and date when two files share a visible name. Keep the historical résumé, retained job writing and observations of browser effects visibly distinct. A legacy record without résumé identity must say so. A missing managed file names the missing version and gives whole-workspace recovery guidance; it never substitutes another file. [Historical row][subui] · [Résumé integrity][resumes] · [Continuation contract][cont]

**Observation-gated build — only what the two-application workflow proves.** Before scheduling any further v0.5 work, run the real two-application workflow on the current build: two related roles, two similarly named résumé files, one reused and tailored answer, one interruption and return. Build a preparation-clarity improvement — for example bringing the chosen résumé, outstanding question and manual letter work into one view, or clearer version/role/date labelling — only for friction an observer actually reproduces there: a wrong-file choice, a hunt for outstanding manual work, a stranded draft. If the existing preparation route already answers “what still needs my judgment on B?” without help, record that finding, cut the redesign, and let v0.5 close early. Do not invent features to fill the milestone.

**Dependency, limits and effort.** Small for the committed retrieval fix; any gated addition is sized by the friction it answers. This requires reliable recorded identity. It can be explored independently of the hiring schema. Defer interactive file repair, PDF comparison, a new profile model, full snapshots of employer submissions and additional ATS engines.

**Acceptance.** Record A using résumé v1. Import a different file with the same visible name as v2, explicitly choose it on B, reuse and tailor an answer for B alone, and return after interruption. Open v1 from A's submission history — including after v1 is archived — and verify the exported bytes match the recorded hash. B still shows v2 and the original approved answer remains available. On a missing-file fixture, historical retrieval identifies A's missing version and supplies no substitute. Separately, file the two-application observation record: each gated improvement either traces to a reproduced friction in that record or is absent from the release.

### v0.6.0 · Ask what needs attention, in your own words, and open the evidence

**Promise and experience.** Ask for due work however it comes to mind — “Which applications need attention?”, “What's on my plate?”, “Anything overdue?” — and get the same dated, actionable results with reasons and links. Ask “Help me prepare for Thursday's interview” and open that application's résumé and relevant saved writing. Then follow up without starting over: “What did I send them?”, “And what's still open on it?” An ambiguous request gets a short clarification — “Thursday has two screens: Northline at 2 and Harbor at 4. Which one?” — not a guess.

**What “conversational” means here, and what it does not.** The supported intentions are bounded: retrieve due/follow-up work, find dated application activity, and retrieve one application's interview material. Naturalness is in the expression, not the scope. The applicant may paraphrase freely, use relative dates (“Thursday,” “last week”), refer to the role, event or result just discussed (“that one,” “it”), and answer a clarification in plain words. The system must resolve those forms to the same grounded local reads the direct UI performs. It must not invent activity, deadlines or employer interest; when “last week” could mean submitted, saved or interviewed, it shows dated matches with their basis or asks. This requires no unrestricted agent autonomy, no general tool platform, no long-lived memory and no parallel state model. The only conversational state is short-lived context for the current interaction — the role and event under discussion and the last result — held as disposable UI state. Closing the conversation loses nothing, because every durable fact already lives in the workspace.

**Reuse and build.** Reuse the due-work view, specific application and material links, and Go's existing preparation decisions. Resolve a supported intention to narrow local reads. Return dates, role IDs, sources and empty/ambiguous results, each with a link or route to the underlying record so the applicant can inspect the evidence behind every claim. Render those same results in conventional navigation. Keep conversation context disposable. A suggestion such as reviewing a saved example should be labelled as preparation advice, with the source available to open. A constrained local interpretation is a reasonable first implementation, provided it is qualified against varied phrasing rather than the phrases its author thought of. Qualify paraphrases, relative dates, contextual references and follow-up chains before claiming conversational coverage. Any optional external interpretation needs disclosure of the exact data and destination, and must still resolve to the same bounded local reads.

**Dependency, limits and effort.** Medium, with uncertainty in how people phrase requests. Requires reliable dates, links and a visible direct equivalent. Use a bounded query for the list. Building a complete workspace snapshot for every job would repeatedly load résumés and verify option bytes in the current path. Check a populated workspace before adding caches or an index. Exclude record writes, document ingestion, generalized semantic search, open-ended chat and invented recent activity. [Workspace read path][workspace] · [Existing bounded letter retrieval][writing]

**Acceptance.** With two Thursday screens, one overdue follow-up, an undated task and a closed posting, ask three different paraphrases of “What's on my plate?” — including one the implementer did not anticipate — and get the same grounded list each time. Open the stated basis for the overdue item. Ask about Thursday, answer the clarification by naming or describing the intended role, and open its exact material. Follow up with “What did I use for it?” and reach the recorded résumé without restating the role. Change the due date visually and ask again. The answer reflects the correction. A query about “the application I worked on last week” either returns matching dated work with its basis or asks for clarification. No query changes a record, and every answer names the records it rests on.

### v0.7.0 · Record an invitation the way you would say it

**Promise and experience.** While looking at an application or the cross-application results, say it the way it arrives: “Phone screen Thursday at two. Add time to prepare Wednesday,” or “They want to talk Thursday afternoon — can we set that up?” or “Screen with Northline, day after tomorrow, need an hour to prep first.” The app resolves the role, local dates, time and any missing details into one editable proposal. Save once and see the event and action alongside the application. A follow-up in the same conversation — “Actually make the prep Tuesday” — revises the proposal before saving, and corrects the saved record through the same proposal afterwards.

**Reuse and build.** Convert supported language into the same draft fields used by v0.4, in whatever varied phrasing the applicant uses. Display concrete calendar dates, time zone, target and the in-app meaning of a reminder. Ask — in the conversation, accepting a plain-language answer — when information is missing or two roles match, and carry the applicant's answers forward so they never repeat a detail already given. Go revalidates the target and current state at confirmation and returns the saved record. If a response is lost, look up the result of that confirmation before retrying. Reuse the direct editor to correct it. Keep the typed sentence available if interpretation fails. Clarification and reference resolution reuse the short-lived conversational context proven in v0.6; confirmation remains the boundary between language and consequence, so flexibility in wording never becomes flexibility in what gets saved without review.

**Dependency, limits and effort.** Medium–high. The costly part is reliable correction across ambiguous names, times and stale records, and honest handling of phrasings the implementation did not anticipate: an unresolvable sentence must fall back to the direct form with the applicant's words intact, never to a guessed save. Build on proven reads and the direct write. Keep writes to interview and linked next-action entry. Defer bulk changes, spoken commands (v0.8), conversational application submission declarations, profile approval, employer Fill and outreach.

**Acceptance.** With two similar roles and an ambiguous “Thursday at two,” the user answers the clarification in their own words, resolves the time, edits the proposed preparation date and confirms. The same invitation phrased three different ways — including one with a relative date and one split across two short messages — produces the same single proposal. A double click or a lost success response yields one event. Canceling creates none. After restart the same corrected item appears in History, due work and conversational results, and a follow-up question about it resolves to the same record. Compare the whole exchange, including repairs and clarifications, with entering the same invitation directly.

### v0.8.0 · Use the workspace unaided, with optional voice for asking and recording

**Promise and experience.** A new applicant can save a role and find it again on a later day. A returning applicant can hold one button and ask “What needs attention today?” and get the same grounded answer typing gives, with the evidence on screen to open. The same button dictates a short invitation: read what the app heard, correct it, and save through the same proposal as typing. Keyboard and pointer controls cover the whole journey, including every voice result.

**Reuse and build.** Use the v0.6 grounded reads, the v0.7 proposal, result and correction flow, and nothing else. Add one opt-in push-to-talk entry with an editable transcript and a visible listening/stop state. A spoken question resolves to the same bounded local read as its typed equivalent and changes no record; a spoken invitation becomes editable text first and reaches the same confirmation as its typed equivalent, so a mishearing is corrected in text before it can become a saved fact. Verify whether speech processing is local on the supported configuration. If it leaves the device, name the recipient, what is transmitted and the retention terms before consent. Improve first-save, return navigation, focus order and save/failure announcements from observed applicant difficulties. Start with a bounded invited cohort.

**Dependency, limits and effort.** High uncertainty in speech accuracy, privacy and whether correction costs outweigh convenience. Voice depends on successful typed conversation; it adds a transcription step, not a new operation. Cohort observation and accessibility work start earlier and can proceed without speech. Exclude always-on listening, audio archives, spoken browser actions, spoken Fill, spoken submission declarations and universal voice navigation. If spoken capture proves unreliable while spoken retrieval is accurate and cheap to correct, ship retrieval alone and describe voice honestly as voice-assisted asking, not voice control. Public beta is a separate audience decision dependent on clean install, a proven upgrade/recovery path, and manageable support.

**Acceptance.** An unfamiliar applicant saves one role without account, AI setup or microphone permission, then returns unaided to the right draft or task. Another applicant asks aloud what needs attention, hears or reads the same dated answer typing produces, and opens its evidence. A third speaks an invitation, spots a wrong employer or time in the transcript, corrects it and saves once. With microphone permission denied or the service unavailable, finish both tasks by keyboard and typing. Observe the status announcements and correction on the installed Mac. Keep any voice half experimental if its privacy or correction journey fails, and do not let a held half block the other.

### v0.9.0 · Upgrade and recover without losing the week’s work

**Promise and experience.** The applicant understands which release they are receiving, chooses when to install/restart, and resumes the same role, files and commitments. If an update fails or optional services are offline, they have a usable local route back.

**Reuse and build.** Use the existing updater, release packaging, stable manifest tooling and complete-workspace backup procedure. Qualify the actual distribution path for the supported audience, including signing/notarization for ordinary public Mac installation. Fix failures in replacement, workspace migration, restoration, and first launch as observed. Verify accessibility and privacy disclosures on the release candidate itself. [Updater host][uphost] · [Published upgrade guidance][r3] · [Release policy][policy]

**Dependency, limits and effort.** High external uncertainty in signing, Gatekeeper and installed upgrade behavior. Continuity checks begin with v0.4. Publishing a newer stable version can reach v0.2 build 5 as well as v0.3, since both check the channel. Before offering it, qualify the installed starting populations it actually reaches or keep the restricted release manual. Exclude new platforms, cloud backups and a custom update service. The launch promise needs an install and upgrade path the team can support.

**Acceptance.** On a clean supported Mac install the downloaded candidate through the advertised path. Separately, start from a supported installed predecessor with two résumé versions, application answers, letter, a recorded submission and hiring work where supported by that predecessor. Decline an offered update and continue working, then approve installation and restart. Compare the actual files, associations, dates and history on reopening. On a failure case, use the documented recovery with the paired database and résumé directory. Employer-browser sessions end on restart and uncertain external effects stay unreplayed.

### v1.0.0 · Manage an application week in one dependable workspace

**Promise and experience.** Complete the week in §7 on a supported installed release. Ask in your own words what needs attention, follow up about the role you mean, and open the evidence behind every answer. Record a short update the same way, by typing or — if qualified — by voice, always through an editable, confirmed proposal. The applicant can always inspect the evidence, finish visually and correct a mistake.

**Reuse and build.** Integrate the qualified slices and close remaining blockers on the repeated journey. Provide clear support, privacy, installation and recovery guidance sized to the advertised Mac and browser operations. Preserve usable local preparation when enrichment, a provider, microphone or employer browser is unavailable. Online posting retrieval and employer interaction need network access.

**Dependency, limits and effort.** Integration and release qualification dominate. Require observed first, repeat and return use, reliable historical retrieval, natural and controllable conversational reads and writes, and the installed platform promise. Recommend a qualified optional voice path — spoken retrieval and spoken capture over the same operations — for the multimodal launch claim. If one voice half falls short, hold that half's claim and return the narrower launch decision to the owner; a typed-only launch remains honest and complete, described as conversational rather than voice-enabled. Defer platform expansion and external automation.

**Acceptance.** An intended applicant on the advertised build can work through §7 after a gap and an update, explain why an item is due, open the right historical file, ask a follow-up that refers to the role just discussed, correct a misheard date and distinguish what was saved, observed in a field, and personally reported. The direct journey also works with optional services disabled. Owner sign-off applies to the exact artifact, scope and observed limitations.

## 7. Intended v1.0 user experience and product identity

Maya is tailoring applications while employers from the previous week start to reply. On Monday she puts an analyst role in Focus and parks a less urgent role. She uses her approved profile, selects a specific résumé revision, and turns an earlier answer into a draft for this employer. The screen shows the outstanding question and the saved letter that needs manual attachment. A change to this application leaves her reusable answer available for the next one.

On Tuesday she opens the employer application, inspects what can be handled, and reviews the destination and included values before authorizing a supported Fill. She completes the remaining controls and submits on the employer site herself. Back in YourMove she records that fact. Later, History can say which résumé her local recording identified and can open its verified bytes. It can show retained answers and a letter as preparation. If the form outcome was uncertain, the record keeps that uncertainty.

On Wednesday she receives a phone-screen invitation. She holds the microphone button, speaks a short update, and spots the wrong time in the transcript. She corrects it, sees Thursday’s actual date and time and Wednesday’s preparation action, and confirms. On Thursday she types “Help me prepare for my screen.” The answer opens her interview note, the résumé associated with the recording and the saved example she chose for the application. She follows up — “What did I send them?” — and reaches the same résumé without naming the role again. If two screens match, she picks the role from a short clarification. A closed posting does not cancel either appointment.

On Friday, hands busy, she holds the same button and asks “What should I follow up on?” She hears the action she set after the screen, its date and the originating application, with the evidence on screen to open. She sends her own follow-up outside YourMove and marks the action done. Next time she opens the app, it has left the due list. She can still find the note and the correct résumé even after importing another revision.

**Why this merits v1.0.** A tracker can store an interview date. Autofill can save keystrokes on a form. YourMove should carry the actual reviewed material and the applicant’s next commitment across that boundary. Its intelligence lies in retrieving the right evidence, explaining outstanding work and turning an instruction into a correctable local proposal. Direct controls are the fastest way to compare files or inspect scope. Conversation helps with cross-application questions phrased however the applicant thinks of them, and voice helps with both asking and short capture when correction is easy. A person who declines both external AI and speech can still do the underlying work.

**Evidence of polish.** Observe intended applicants use the installed build for a first role, a related second role and a return on another day. Ask them to find the exact file, explain an item’s due date and distinguish employer-side action from local recording. Watch for wrong-role choices, unnecessary re-entry, observer help, stranded drafts and time spent repairing language input. Record the build, journey and failure, fix the obstacle and repeat the affected task. Check keyboard focus, screen-reader save/error feedback, narrow-window operation and responsiveness with a populated workspace. Widen distribution only when those tasks and the advertised install/upgrade are workable unaided. A confirmed wrong-target Fill, silent data loss or unexpected disclosure blocks the affected launch claim. [Behavioral reference][bdd] · [Release policy][policy]

## 10. Adopted roadmap summary

**Product promise.** Return to the right application, recover the material you reviewed, and see what you decided to do next. Work visually, ask in your own words and follow up naturally, or speak a short question or update. You decide what leaves the workspace and personally submit every application.

| Release | Applicant outcome |
|---|---|
| **v0.4.0** | Record an interview and a dated next action from a specific application. Find it on return, correct it, and preserve it through restart and correction of a mistaken submission record. |
| **v0.5.0** | Open the exact résumé a past application used, straight from its submission record. Any further preparation clarity is limited to friction a real second application reproduces. |
| **v0.6.0** | Ask what needs attention in your own words, clarify which role you mean, follow up without restating it, and open the evidence behind every answer. |
| **v0.7.0** | Turn an invitation phrased your way into one confirmed, editable interview and next action. |
| **v0.8.0** | Complete first and return visits unaided in a bounded beta. Qualify optional push-to-talk for both asking what needs attention and recording an invitation, on the same operations and correction path. |
| **v0.9.0** | Install, upgrade, recover and continue on the advertised Mac configuration with the same records and files. |
| **v1.0.0** | Complete the preparation-to-follow-up week in a dependable local-first workspace with qualified visual, natural conversational and optional voice operation. |

This summary establishes product direction. Each release still requires a bounded scope decision, observed installed behavior and owner authorization to ship.
