How generation and privacy work

Answer proposals stay unsaved until you adopt and explicitly save them. Cover-letter generation creates a separate local draft for review.

YourMove builds one prompt from your saved job description, master resume, and chosen role resume. Generation only starts when you approve it. External services may send that evidence off-device; custom endpoints depend on your configuration, and some Ollama models use cloud inference. Existing drafts are never replaced automatically.

CLI integrations use your separately installed, unmodified vendor tools. A detected login is not a guarantee of remaining subscription allowance. API keys and usage are separate from ChatGPT and Claude subscriptions. Claude subscription use in a distributed third-party product may require additional permission.
