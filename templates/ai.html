How writing works

YourMove builds one prompt from your saved job description, master resume, and chosen role resume. Generation only starts when you approve it. Cloud and subscription-backed options send that evidence off-device; Ollama runs locally. Existing drafts are never replaced automatically.

CLI integrations use your separately installed, unmodified vendor tools. A detected login is not a guarantee of remaining subscription allowance. API keys and usage are separate from ChatGPT and Claude subscriptions. Claude subscription use in a distributed third-party product may require additional permission.
