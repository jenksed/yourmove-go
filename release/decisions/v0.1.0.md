## Scope and known limits

- macOS Apple Silicon only, macOS 26+.
- Application-browser selector offers Chromium and Firefox (stored preference, env override preserved). WebKit is not offered; on cache-less Macs Firefox shows unavailable with reason.
- Career Discovery and Career Studio are parked behind `careerDiscoveryEnabled`; Career Evidence is unaffected and used by cover letters.
- Assisted employer-form operation supports qualified fixtures; live ATS compatibility is untested and unsupported fields stay manual.
- AI providers advertised only after consent; unconfigured providers fail clearly without touching saved work.
- No automatic employer submission, no Windows/Linux/Intel release, no auto-updater commitment.
- Local ad-hoc signing is not Developer ID signing or Apple notarization.
- NOT VERIFIED independently in this record: Safari surface, native picker cancel, clean-install procedure/first-launch Gatekeeper detail (owner reports completion).
