## Scope and known limits

- macOS Apple Silicon only; macOS 26+.
- Chrome/Chromium required for assisted employer-form operation.
- No universal ATS compatibility; unsupported fields remain manual.
- AI providers advertised only after consent and responsiveness qualification.
- No automatic employer submission, no Windows/Linux/Intel release, no auto-updater commitment.
- Local ad-hoc signing is not Developer ID signing or Apple notarization.
